Bonus tips from Maurice Rickard:

Drone out on B with the A string open, and alternately fingering the E string and leaving it open. This gives you the open-tuning feel of original in the outro.

For an extra bit of flair, play this right before the D in the chorus instead of playing the last iteration of the riff:
