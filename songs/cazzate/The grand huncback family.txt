
The grand hunchback family

Verse 1
An old man and his sweetheart,
ninety winters, grey and wise,
bent like reeds along the river
under heavy, troubled skies.
An old man and his sweetheart,
ninety summers drifting by,
went to wed beneath the steeple
tears were running down their eyes.

Chorus
Humped was his father, humped was her mother,
and his sister’s daughter, bless her soul,
curled like a prawn in a fisherman’s bowl.

Hump on the father, hump on the mother,
hump on the niece from head to toe,
the grand hunchback family, oh!

Verse 2
When the wedding bells would chime,
the band struck up a merry tune;
they all swayed and bent in time,
like the crescent of the moon.

Verse 3
And the priest who tied the knot,
was the crookedest in town,
when he raised his hands to bless,
took a nosedive to the ground.

After nine moons come and gone,
a little boy drew breath, and there,
a tiny hump upon his back:
the family’s proud and perfect heir!

Final chorus
Humped was his father, humped was her mother,
and his sister’s daughter, bless her soul,
curled like a prawn in a fisherman’s bowl.

Hump on the father, hump on the mother,
hump on the baby, row on row,
the big-ass-hump family, oh!
